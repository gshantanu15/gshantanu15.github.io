01

Independent project · AI and voice systems

Voice Agent Lab

A consent-first bilingual voice-agent prototype for real-estate lead qualification.

Consented evaluation

Bilingual English–Hindi demo

Hear the conversation design in practice.

Recorded with Sarvam AI. Retell AI powers the separate outbound-call Python integration.

RETELL AISARVAM AICONSENT FIRST

Context

Outbound voice agents need to qualify leads without losing consent, conversational flexibility, or control over the facts they communicate.

Providers

Retell AI is used for the outbound-call Python integration. Sarvam AI was used for the published conversational evaluations shown here.

My role

I developed the prototype and its conversation contract for bilingual English-Hindi lead qualification.

What I did

  • Designed permission-first and out-of-order qualification flows.
  • Created retrieval-backed behavior grounded in a curated knowledge base.
  • Added opt-out, formatting, safety, and structured outcome rules.
  • Optionally supported validation, secret-safe errors, dependency injection, and network-free tests.

Result

The repository includes an executable prototype and consented evaluations covering normal, bilingual, early-exit, retrieval, and adversarial conversations. The audio demo above was recorded with Sarvam AI and is not a recording of the Retell integration.

  • Python
  • Retell AI
  • Sarvam AI
  • Conversation design
  • Retrieval
  • Safety and testing
